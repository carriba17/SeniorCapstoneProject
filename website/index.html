The Luxury Resale Market is Massive: NFTs Eliminate Fakes

The U.S. luxury resale market is valued at $8.65 billion and projected to reach $13.04 billion by 2030. However, counterfeiting remains a critical challenge, costing brands billions and eroding consumer trust. Through blockchain-based NFTs, companies can create immutable digital certificates of authenticity, permanently eliminating fakes and revolutionizing how luxury goods are verified, traded, and owned. The fast-growing luxury resale market is reshaping ecommerce and sustainability.
